Open for the season

La Terrazza

The terrace is open for the season: lunch and dinner served outdoors, with seasonal dishes, cold wine, and the long Surrey evening doing the rest. Book ahead for dinner; the best tables go quickly on a warm night.

Plan your visit
Every day · 10am to 4pm

Sicilian Brunch

A Sicilian sharing table, every day from 10am to 4pm. Order the trios (tris) and pass the plates: arancini, bruschetta, fresh pasta, grills, cocktails and dolci, all made to share. Walk-ins are welcome, just arrive hungry.
